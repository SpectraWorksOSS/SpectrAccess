# EUMETSAT MSG SEVIRI data terms

Source collections: MSG SEVIRI Level 1.5 full disc
`EO:EUM:DAT:MSG:HRSEVIRI` and rapid scan `EO:EUM:DAT:MSG:MSG15-RSS`.
The [EUMETSAT Data Policy](https://www.eumetsat.int/eumetsat-data-policy)
distinguishes Core Data and Recommended Data. Its applicable data classification,
latency and account licence determine access, use and redistribution rights.

**Collection-specific MSG terms and classification have not yet been confirmed
for these two collections.** Successful account access is not evidence of
unrestricted redistribution. Consult EUMETSAT and the licence actually granted
to your account before sharing original native files, extracted numerical
arrays, derived products or catalogue metadata. This connector downloads on
the user's behalf and grants no additional rights to any of those outputs.

The [policy PDF](https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-01/45173%20-%20Data_Policy.pdf)
(Articles 4-6 and 14) distinguishes attribution, original numerical-data
redistribution and Value Added Services. Core Data under CC-BY-4.0 and licensed
Recommended Data have different conditions; neither route is asserted here
for either MSG collection. A Recommended Meteosat licence can permit use for
any purpose while restricting redistribution of original numerical data.
Check your collection's actual classification and licence.

Provider attribution form for Meteosat data is
"EUMETSAT Meteosat data/product [Year]"; for modified output use
"Contains modified EUMETSAT Meteosat data/product [Year]".
Retain product identity, collection, source and retrieval time, and identify
modifications. Attribution alone does not grant redistribution permission.
See [EUMETSAT data licensing](https://user.eumetsat.int/resources/user-guides/eumetsat-data-licensing)
and your account's licence text for the applicable conditions.
