# EUMETSAT FCI data terms

Source: EUMETSAT Meteosat Third Generation FCI L1c FDHSI and L2 cloud products.
The [EUMETSAT Data Policy](https://www.eumetsat.int/eumetsat-data-policy)
distinguishes Core Data from Recommended Data.

Retrospective FCI Level 1 data with latency of at least one hour and FCI
derived Level 2 products are Core Data under CC-BY-4.0, with attribution.
Use attribution such as "EUMETSAT Meteosat data/product [year]" or
"Contains modified EUMETSAT Meteosat data/product [year]" for modified data.
Preserve product provenance and identify modifications in redistributed results.
Check the current provider policy before redistribution or operational use.

FCI Level 1 data with latency below one hour are Recommended Data and have
different access, licence and redistribution conditions. This connector is
intended for retrospective full-disc use; access credentials do not grant
rights beyond the provider's terms.

The connector code is Apache-2.0. The optional Satpy 0.60.0 dependency is
GPLv3 or later, and EUMDAC is MIT. Those software licences are separate from
the EUMETSAT observation-data licence.
