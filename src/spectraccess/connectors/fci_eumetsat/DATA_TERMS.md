# EUMETSAT FCI data terms

Source: EUMETSAT Meteosat Third Generation FCI L1c FDHSI and L2 cloud products.
The [EUMETSAT Data Policy](https://www.eumetsat.int/eumetsat-data-policy)
distinguishes Core Data from Recommended Data.

Hourly FCI Level 1 data and FCI derived Level 2 products are Core Data
under CC-BY-4.0, with attribution (Data Policy Articles 4-6).
Use attribution such as "EUMETSAT Meteosat data/product [year]" or
"Contains modified EUMETSAT Meteosat data/product [year]" for modified data.
Preserve product provenance and identify modifications in redistributed results.
Check the current provider policy before redistribution or operational use.

The 10-minute FCI Level 1c repeat cycles fetched by this connector are
Recommended Data. Access with latency of at least one hour is Without Charge
for any use under Article 14.1, but this does not confer unrestricted
redistribution rights. Article 6.2 prohibits redistribution of original
numerical Recommended Data except as expressly authorized by the policy
or EUMETSAT. Article 14.3 describes limited licensed redistribution, while
Article 14.4 permits service providers to supply Value Added Services.
Below one hour latency, different access fees apply. This connector is
intended for retrospective full-disc use; access credentials do not grant
rights beyond the provider's terms. Consult the
[policy PDF](https://www-cdn.eumetsat.int/files/2026-01/45173%20-%20Data_Policy.pdf)
before sharing source numerical data.

The connector code is Apache-2.0. The optional Satpy 0.60.0 dependency is
GPLv3 or later, and EUMDAC is MIT. Those software licences are separate from
the EUMETSAT observation-data licence.
