# NASA VIIRS data terms

NASA Earth Science data accessed through EOSDIS are openly available.
An Earthdata account is needed for authenticated downloads from LAADS DAAC.
spectrAccess fetches files on the user's behalf; it does not redistribute
provider files. Synthetic fixtures contain no downloaded observations.

Cite the NASA VIIRS product and collection used, its DOI, LAADS DAAC,
platform, access date, and granule identifiers. Acknowledge NASA EOSDIS
and the product science teams. CMR collection metadata and product landing
pages supply each collection's current citation and DOI. For example,
SNPP MOD L1B Collection 2 is DOI `10.5067/VIIRS/VNP02MOD.002` and
geolocation is `10.5067/VIIRS/VNP03MOD.002`.

- [NASA Earthdata data-use policy](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-and-software/data-and-information-policy)
- [LAADS DAAC](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/)
- [SNPP MOD L1B Collection 2](https://doi.org/10.5067/VIIRS/VNP02MOD.002)
- [SNPP MOD geolocation Collection 2](https://doi.org/10.5067/VIIRS/VNP03MOD.002)

Satpy 0.60.0 in the optional `viirs` extra is GPLv3+.
spectrAccess source remains Apache-2.0. NASA data policy and software
licensing are separate; consult the dependency licence for distribution.
