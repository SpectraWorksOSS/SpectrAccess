# Sentinel-3 SLSTR data terms

Sentinel-3 SLSTR data are European Union Copernicus Sentinel data,
distributed through the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem. ESA and EUMETSAT
operate and process Sentinel-3 products. Access through CDSE requires
a registered account for downloads.

Use the attribution `Contains Copernicus Sentinel data [year]` for
redistributed provider data and `Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel
data [year]` for modified data. Replace `[year]` with the acquisition year.
Retain source identifiers and processing/collection versions. Attribution
does not imply endorsement by the European Union, ESA or EUMETSAT.

See the [Copernicus Sentinel legal notice](https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/documents/247904/690755/Sentinel_Data_Legal_Notice)
and [CDSE terms](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions).
Users are responsible for complying with current provider terms.

The optional `slstr` extra includes Satpy 0.60.0, licensed GPLv3 or later.
spectrAccess remains Apache-2.0. Consult Satpy's licence for redistribution
of software incorporating that dependency.
