# Data terms

Observation data retain their providers' terms. Connector software licences
do not replace source data licences. Consult each connector's DATA_TERMS.md
under `src/spectraccess/connectors/` before using or redistributing data.

For MTG-FCI, hourly Level 1 data and derived Level 2 products are EUMETSAT
Core Data under CC-BY-4.0. Attribution is
required: "EUMETSAT Meteosat data/product [year]", or "Contains modified
EUMETSAT Meteosat data/product [year]" for modified data. The 10-minute
Level 1c cycles fetched by the FCI connector are Recommended Data, including
retrospective data. Access after at least one hour is Without Charge, but
redistribution of original numerical data remains restricted by the policy.
See [FCI data terms](src/spectraccess/connectors/fci_eumetsat/DATA_TERMS.md).
