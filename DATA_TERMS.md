# Data terms

Observation data retain their providers' terms. Connector software licences
do not replace source data licences. Consult each connector's DATA_TERMS.md
under `src/spectraccess/connectors/` before using or redistributing data.

For MTG-FCI, retrospective Level 1 data with latency of at least one hour
and Level 2 products are EUMETSAT Core Data under CC-BY-4.0. Attribution is
required: "EUMETSAT Meteosat data/product [year]", or "Contains modified
EUMETSAT Meteosat data/product [year]" for modified data. FCI Level 1 data
under one hour latency have different licence and redistribution conditions.
See [FCI data terms](src/spectraccess/connectors/fci_eumetsat/DATA_TERMS.md).
